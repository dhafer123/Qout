# Vegetable stickers

15 stickers (asparagus to tomato; the welcome pages' spinach, pepper and
garlic were removed in T44b), cut from a sticker sheet supplied by
Dhafer Romdhani on 2026-11-23, who holds the rights to use them in Qout.

Each sticker was cut from the sheet's cream background into a transparent PNG
(about 200 px, so shown at 72 dp or smaller). Not covered by the repository's
code licence.

`onionTear.png` (a smiling red onion with a tear, shown on every empty state)
was supplied separately by Dhafer Romdhani on 2026-11-24, who holds the
rights to use it in Qout. It was cut from its white background into a
transparent PNG and cropped (158 × 220 px).
